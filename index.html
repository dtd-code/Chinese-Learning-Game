语音和清晰度
Pronunciation and clarity
  1. 5分：语音非常准确和清晰。
    Clear and consistently good pronunciation with hardly any errors.
  2. 4分：语音相当准确，有少许错误。语音相当清晰。
    Clear pronunciation with occasional errors that do not affect intelligibility.
  3. 3分：语音还算准确，有较多错误，不太清晰。
    Generally clear pronunciation with several errors that are noticeable.
  4. 2分：语音不太准确，有相当多错误。语音不太清晰，某些语音难以识别。
    Pronunciation somewhat difficult to understand, with numerous serious errors.
  5. 1分：语音不准确，大多数字都读错。语音不清晰。
    Very unclear pronunciation, with most words being mispronounced.
语调和流利程度
Intonation and level of fluency
  1. 5分：非常流利。语速适中，停顿适当。语调正确有节奏感。
    Fluent reading delivered with appropriate pauses and without hesitations. Appropriate variation of pitch and tone.
  2. 4分：相当流利。语速相当适中，甚少迟疑。语调相当正确，有适当的变化。
    Smooth delivery with almost no hesitations. Some variation of appropriate pitch and tone.
  3. 3分：还算流利。语速还算适中，较多迟疑。语调还算正确，有些许变化。
    Generally smooth delivery with some hesitations. A slight attempt to vary pitch and tone.
  4. 2分：不太流利。语速不太适中，停顿不太适当。语调不太正确。
    Generally uneven delivery with a number of noticeable hesitations.
  5. 1分：不流利。语速不适中，停顿不适当。语调不正确，没有变化。
    Very slow and jerky delivery, almost word-by-word pronunciation.
